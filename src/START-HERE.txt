RGSX Library Checker {VERSION}
==============================

Shows the games you have installed in RetroBat or Batocera, compares each
system with Top 10 to Top 500 lists, and suggests top free games.

HOW TO USE

1. Copy "rgsx-library-checker.html" into your RetroBat folder
   (for example C:\RetroBat), or the Batocera "share" folder.

2. Double-click it to open it in Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge.
   (Firefox and Safari can't open folders from a web page.)
   If it opens in another browser, right-click the file > Open with > Chrome.

3. Click "Choose this page's folder..." and choose the folder you copied it
   into. Click "View files", then "Save changes" / "Allow".

That's it. The page finds your roms folder and the RGSX database by itself.
Settings > Setup check shows what it found. Click Help for everything else.

BATOCERA

Batocera runs on its own machine. From your computer open its share folder
over the network, copy the page into it, and choose the share folder:
- Windows: in File Explorer type \\BATOCERA\share
- macOS:   Finder > Go > Connect to Server > smb://batocera.local/share
- Linux:   file manager > Other Locations > smb://batocera.local/share

BROWSERS

Works in Chrome, Edge, Chromium, Opera and Vivaldi on Windows, macOS and Linux.
Firefox, Safari, phones and tablets can't open folders: the page warns you.

WHAT'S IN THE "extras" FOLDER

You don't need these to start. The page can copy them for you too.
- list-grabber.js : imports a top list from a website (Settings > a system).
- itch-refresh.js : refreshes the free itch.io games list.

FILES THE PAGE CREATES (next to it)

- rgsx-library-checker.config.json  your settings (API keys are encrypted)
- rgsx-library-checker-cache\       caches; safe to delete

It never changes your ROMs or the RGSX database.
